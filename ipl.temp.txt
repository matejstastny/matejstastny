CONSTITUTION

Industry Project Lab at the University of Washington Bothell



Article I.	Name

This organization shall be known as the Industry Project Lab (IPL) at the University of Washington Bothell.



Article II.	Mission

It is the mission of the Peanut Butter & Jelly Club at the University of Washington Bothell to promote interest and awareness of peanut butter and jelly sandwich opportunities within the campus community through the use of films, lectures, outings, and other events.



It is the mission of the Industry Project Lab Club at the University of Washington Bothell to promote interest and awareness of industry-related opportunities within the campus community, through the use of films, lectures, outings, and other events. Industry Project Lab strives to bridge the divide between the workforce and students. It hopes to create a space where students can network and gain hands-on industry-related experience.



Films means content



Article III.	Affiliations (IF APPLICABLE)

We, the Peanut Butter & Jelly Club, are a chapter of the National Student Peanut Butter & Jelly Organization and are here to continue their mission and further their values here at UWB.



Article IV. 	Membership

Membership of the Industry Project Lab Club is open to all, in accordance with University policy. 

This organization and its members shall not discriminate against any individual(s) because of race, color, creed, religion, national origin, citizenship, sex, pregnancy, age, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, genetic information, disability, or veteran status.



Article V.	Meetings

Section 1      Quorum

A meeting shall be declared to have a quorum if a simple majority of the club membership is present.



Section 2      Officers

The executive board shall meet at least twice a month during the regular school year.



Section 3      Club Meetings

Meetings shall be held monthly starting in August during the regular school year.  To remain in good standing, a member is expected to attend at least 10 (ten) of the regularly scheduled weekly meetings.



Meetings shall be held on a weekly basis starting in September during the regular school year. To remain in good standing, a member is expected to attend at least seven (7) meetings per academic quarter, and the majority of club events.



Article VI.	Officers



Section 1	Officer Selection

The officers shall be re-elected by ballot at the meeting prior to Spring Break in the Spring Quarter.

The person with a simple majority of the vote will be declared the winner.



 Section 2	Officer Removal

Impeachment charges may be initiate against any officer by any member of the club in writing stating the charges:

Violation of the Industry Project Lab Constitution

Abuse of powers

Neglect of duties

The charges shall be read by the presiding officer at the next meeting, which must be held within seven school days following submission of the charges.

The recommendation for impeachment shall be taken up as the first item of business.

The officer being impeached shall be removed from office if two-thirds (2/3) of the current membership votes for removal of the said officer.



Section 3        Officers

The officers of the Industry Project Lab shall be President, Vice President (2), Treasurer, Marketing & Communications, Communications Lead, Technical Lead, and General Officer (2).



Section 4      Duties of Office



President

Enforce the Constitution of the Industry Project Lab Club.

Preside over all Industry Project Lab Club meetings with an impartial demeanor.

Represent Industry Project Lab Club on all public relations and official functions.

Delegate responsibilities and oversee execution of all club initiatives

Prepare and post the agenda and minutes of each meeting

Note club updates in Industry Project Lab Notion page, following each meeting

Maintain accurate attendance and activity/event records including all club correspondence (Email, Mail Box)

Responsible for attending Club Council Officer training





Vice President of External Relations

Assume all responsibilities of the President when necessary.

Maintain all applicable Calendars and Web information.

Lead external outreach communications

Maintain relationships with partner companies and organizations

Take meeting notes and maintain club records

Manage GitHub and repository documentation







Vice President of Internal Relations

Assume all responsibilities of the President when necessary.

Maintain all applicable Calendars and Web information.

Take meeting notes and maintain club records.

Manage schedules and timelines for club initiatives.

Coordinate Quarterly board social events

Responsible for attending Club Council Officer training



Secretary 

Will preside over Industry Project Lab meetings in the absence of the President and Vice President.

Prepare and post agenda and minutes of each meeting.

Email updated minutes to club members following each meeting.

Maintain accurate attendance and activity/event records including all club correspondence (Email, Voice Mail, Mail Box)

Responsible for attending Club Council Officer training



Treasurer

Receive, record, and deposit all Industry Project Lab funds.

Be responsible for requesting club funding from Club Council.

Maintain accurate fiscal records.

Present financial updates to executive board for final approval

Collaborate with executive board to allocate budget for events and student projects

Responsible for filling out budget requests for all expenditures and events

Responsible for attending Club Council Officer training.



Marketing & Communications

Act as liaison between Industry Project Lab and all other campus offices and organizations

Maintain the Industry Project Lab social media accounts, including Discord & Instagram

Create and oversee design outreach materials and promotional content

Maintain brand consistency across all club materials and platforms

Create a club content calendar for uploading and sending out campus outreach





Communications Lead

Create and customize outreach emails/templates

Work with executive board to create outreach strategy for partnerships

Attend outreach meeting for potential club events

Track outreach emails & responses and adjust outreach strategy





Technical Lead

Track materials and equipment needed for club

Help student teams with project questions

Manage GitHub and repository documentation

Understand the technical scope and capabilities of club projects, and communicate it to club board

Coordinate with Treasurer on hardware and parts needed



General Officer (2)

Support club initiatives and projects

Assist board members with assigned tasks and responsibilities

Help onboard and mentor new club members

Substitute for other club officers when needed



Section 5      Terms of Office

One term of office for each executive board member, shall be three (3) (2) quarters.  There shall be no limit imposed on the number of terms any member may serve.





Section 6      Vacancies of Office

If a vacancy exists for any position, then a special election shall be held for the vacant position.  In the interim then succession of office will be used to fill the position until elections can be held for that position.  Succession of office is as follows: President, Vice-President, Secretary, and Treasurer.



Article VII.	Amendements

Amendments to the Constitution of the Industry Project Lab Club must be tabled for discussion for a minimum of one week, and then votes will be taken on all new amendments. Amendments will be ratified by the approval of the membership by two-thirds (2/3) of members in a meeting that has declared a quorum.





Article IX. 	Optional Sections (Ex. Dues, Bylaws, Committees, Ratification, etc.)



Section 1: Electronic Communication Guidelines

Members are expected to check Discord and email regularly for club updates and announcements.

Respond to official club announcements within 48 hours during the academic school year.

Board members should respond to direct messages and inquiries within 24 hours when possible. Exceptions may be made for emergencies or unforeseen circumstances beyond your control.

All communications should maintain a respectful and professional tone.
